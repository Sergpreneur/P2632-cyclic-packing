# Literature audit (initial, not exhaustive)

Date: 2026-10-16.

**TheoremDB P2632:** The current visible statement retains `9 <= M <= 13` and a nine-base-block witness. The 2026-07-25 research packet explicitly reports 145 triple orbits and 4761 internally compatible 5-set orbits, and describes a Z3 run for target 13 as inconclusive. Our 12-base-block witness is therefore a strict improvement **relative to the displayed TheoremDB record**.

Web source: https://theoremdb.org/statements/P2632/

**R. F. Bailey & A. C. Burgess (2013):** Generalized packing designs, *Discrete Mathematics* 313, 1167-1190. General design framework / pair-incidence upper bound, not directly a witness for these parameters.

**F. R. K. Chung, J. A. Salehi & V. K. Wei (1989):** Optical orthogonal codes: design, analysis and applications, *IEEE Trans. Inform. Theory* 35, 595-604. Standard OOC correspondence.

**W. Chu & C. J. Colbourn (2004):** Optimal (n,4,2)-OOC of small orders, *Discrete Mathematics* 279, 163-172. Related exact-search setting but weight four rather than five.

**S. Ma & Y. Chang (2005):** Constructions of optimal optical orthogonal codes with weight five, *J. Combin. Designs* 13, 54-69. The accessible abstract focuses on (gv,5,1) codes, **different correlation parameter**. https://doi.org/10.1002/jcd.20022

**M. Buratti, A. Pasotti & D. Wu (2013):** On optimal (v,5,2,1) optical orthogonal codes, *Designs, Codes and Cryptography* 68, 349-371. Notation distinguishes autocorrelation 2 from cross-correlation 1; **not** the (31,5,2,2) code studied here. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10623-012-9654-x

**Y. M. Chee (2026):** A G-Invariant Erdős--Hanani Theorem with Applications to Optical Orthogonal and Constant-Weight Codes, arXiv:2610.10973, posted October 7, 2026; gives asymptotic results, no particular exact M(31,5,3) claim found in the abstract. https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10973

The precise priority claim "first packing of 12 orbits worldwide" is **not established** by these checks. Before preprint submission, obtain and inspect the entire small-parameter OOC tables and all references reporting Phi(31,5,2), including supplementary tables.
